## I: Defining Friendship

For a long time, I didn't bother to define the word 'friend' precisely. As a result, I fell into the common belief that
friends were simply the people I hung around with, studied with and did activities with. I've come to believe that
this phenomenon is better captured by the term 'proximity'.

While proximity is certainly an important element of friendship, it isn't sufficient by itself, at least in the way I
define the word 'friend'. I consider a friend to be someone I can have insightful conversations with in at least one
domain or discipline. Whether or not you agree with this definition is entirely up to you, but keeping it in mind will
help you understand my perspective on making friends at university.
